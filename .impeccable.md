## Design Context

### Users
F1 followers exploring driver records and the stories behind the numbers. The current feature starts with Kimi Antonelli's career and 2026 season.

### Brand Personality
Informed, lively, celebratory. The creator explicitly wants the stats page to be fun.

### Aesthetic Direction
Continue F1 Bulletin's established typography, navigation, and themes. For the stats collection, use an editorial record-book treatment with chronological entries and a distinct driver cover. The creator confirmed a playful record book matching the site.

### Design Principles
- Make record claims precise and sourceable.
- Keep season achievements distinct from career history.
- Celebrate through typography and storytelling, while preserving clear reading order.
- Keep navigation and controls usable on mobile and by keyboard.
- Label curated snapshots with their coverage date.
